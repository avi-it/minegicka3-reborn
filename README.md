# Minegicka III Reborn

An unofficial, open-source reimplementation of **Minegicka III** for modern Minecraft (Java 26.3, Fabric).
Queue up to five of eight elements, combine them, and cast sprays, beams, lightning, boulders, shields and
magicks, just like in the original.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) 0.19.5+ for Minecraft 26.3 (Java 25).
2. Put `minegicka3-reborn-<version>.jar` and Fabric API 0.162.0+26.3 in `.minecraft/mods`.
3. Start the Fabric 26.3 profile.

## How to play (hold a staff)

- **Elements:** `Y` Water, `U` Life, `I` Shield, `N` Cold, `H` Lightning, `J` Arcane, `K` Earth, `M` Fire
  (rebindable under Controls). Water + Cold = Ice, Water + Fire = Steam. Opposites cancel out
  (Fire/Cold, Arcane/Life, Lightning/Earth or Water, Shield/Shield).
- **Hold right click** to cast forward, **Shift + right click** to cast around you (Area),
  **hold left click** to cast on yourself (Self).
- **R** casts a magick if the queue matches one, **Shift + R** clears the queue.
- **Right click with an empty queue** uses the staff's special ability
  (Blessing, Destruction, Telekinesis, Manipulation, Hemmy).
- **Spell shape:** Shield makes shields, walls, mines and storms; Earth or Ice throws boulders or icicles;
  Arcane or Life fires a beam; Lightning chains; everything else sprays.

**Magicks** (order matters): Haste HAF, Nullify AD, Teleport HAH, Lightning Bolt SHAH, Explosion HFAF,
Vortex IAIDI, Homing Lightning SSHAH, Extinguish WD, De-potion LD, Feather Fall SD, Freeze Motion ID,
Gravitational ED, Snow CD, Thaw FD, Water Shock HD, Collect SEES
(A Arcane, C Cold, D Shield, E Earth, F Fire, H Lightning, I Ice, L Life, S Steam, W Water).

All recipes use the normal crafting table (see the recipe book). Thingy = iron ingot + gold ingot.

### Not ported yet

The magic crafting station and staff enchanting, the Magickpedia, the unlock system, the 888 boss and its
towers, the Mage mob, dungeon loot, and hats on the player's head (they still work as items).

### Original look (optional, personal use)

`tools/make_original_pack.py` builds a resource pack that puts the original HUD icons back, read from
**your own copy** of the original 1.7.10 jar. Those icons belong to WilliamEze, so please don't redistribute
the generated pack.

## Building

Requires JDK 25.

```
./gradlew build             # jar in build/libs
./gradlew runGametest       # headless spell tests
./gradlew runClientGametest # client screenshots in build/run/clientGameTest/screenshots
```

Development notes are in [`MODLOG.md`](MODLOG.md).
